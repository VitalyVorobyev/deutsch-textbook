# Editorial handoff

Report:

- reading id, source hash and local Studio URL;
- narration profile and narrator versions;
- word, paragraph and cue counts;
- duration and voiced words per second;
- full and worst paragraph WER;
- narrator-consistency warning and any pronunciation overrides;
- explicit statement that human approval and publication remain pending.

The editor must listen for long-form coherence, sentence stress, direct speech, CEFR clarity, fatigue, clicks and joins.

# A1–B1 topic-quality audit ledger

This ledger tracks the bounded, topic-by-topic content pass required by P27-3g. It uses the
[content-quality audit contract](../authoring/content-quality-audit.md) and covers the whole topic
profile: article, outcomes, teaching sequence, delayed probes, readings, audio/documents and owned
vocabulary.

`Pass complete` means those surfaces were inspected together and material defects found in the pass
were corrected. It is **not** a claim that a human editor approved a draft topic, nor that learners
have mastered it. The manifest's `status` and provenance records remain the approval source of
truth.

## Deferred or deliberately unchanged in A1

- No recording script was changed. `ls-wohnen-01` is terse, but *Ich nehme sie* is possible in the
  stated situation; a stylistic preference does not justify breaking script/audio provenance.
- Identical RU/UK gloss strings such as *вода*, *метро* and *банк* were sampled and retained where
  the two languages genuinely share the contextual form. Equality is a review signal, not an error.
- Previously reviewed topics keep their recorded status. The six draft topics do not become reviewed
  merely because this bounded pass and automated gates succeed.

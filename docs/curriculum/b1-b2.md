# B1–B2 curriculum contract

What B2 teaches, which unit owns what, and what each unit may not touch. The thirteen units are
under [Units](#units), in path order, in the format of [`a2-b1.md`](a2-b1.md#b1) (Mission, Grammar,
Exclude). The identity rules there apply unchanged: an id is frozen the moment it ships, and until
then the id written here is a working name.

## Anchors

DTZ ends at B1, so none of the DTZ Themen, Sprachhandlungen or Strukturen lists reach B2. **No
free, formal B2 inventory of grammar or vocabulary exists**: the Goethe *Prüfungsziele* delegates
both to *Profile deutsch*, a purchased book this course does not hold; telc declines to list
either; the BAMF goal catalogue states that it gives no grammatical or lexical progression. B2's
denominators are therefore compiled from the free documents below
([ADR 0024](../adrs/0024-b2-denominators-are-compiled-from-free-sources.md)). The files are local
and gitignored ([ADR 0009](../adrs/0009-third-party-and-official-material.md)); the repository
carries labels only.

What each denominator is, and what it is not:

- **Themen** are the 14 Prüfungsthemen: Persönliche Daten und Verhältnisse; Wohnen, Umwelt;
  tägliches Leben, Arbeit; Freizeit, Unterhaltung; Reise; Beziehungen zu anderen Menschen, Kultur,
  Tradition; Gesundheit und Hygiene; Erziehung, Ausbildung, Lernen; Konsum, Handel; Ernährung;
  Dienstleistungen; Orte; Sprache, Kommunikation; Klima. A unit claims them in its topic manifest,
  and `bun scripts/themen.ts B2` reports which are still `unclaimed`. This is the one B2
  denominator an exam publishes.
- **Structures** are measured against three syllabi in counts, never as a percentage
  ([ADR 0025](../adrs/0025-b2-structures-are-held-against-three-syllabi.md)):
  `bun scripts/structures.ts B2` prints how many of the 75 entries (28 + 20 + 27, read off its
  `source:` lines) an inventory row claims. The lists overlap, so 75 counts labels, not
  structures. A syllabus is evidence that a structure is taught at B2 and says nothing about what
  it leaves out; a structure two or three of them name is common practice at the level. What this
  course teaches that none of them names is listed under
  [Beyond the syllabus](#beyond-the-syllabus), and each such row is `beyond` in the report,
  visibly.
- **Sprachhandlungen** have no transcribed denominator yet. Until CON-12 lands, a unit's outcomes
  are held to the six production functions below, and no Sprachhandlungen figure is published for
  B2.
- **Vocabulary** has no denominator and will not get one: no B2 word list is published, and the
  frequency list that could stand in (DeReWo) may be passed on only with its documentation and
  never commercially. A B2 deck makes no coverage claim.

## Progression principles

- **B2 grows abstraction and nuance, not paradigm count.** The *Prüfungsziele* name three marks of
  B2 against B1 (§4.5): effective argumentation, staying power in discourse, and language
  awareness. Texts move beyond the concrete everyday topics of B1 to abstract ones.
- **B2 deepens B1 and never re-teaches it.** `deepens` edges and cumulative retrieval work as in
  B1. A structure that B1 holds receptively is produced at B2, starting with Konjunktiv I in
  reported speech (`data/grammar-inventory.yaml`, row `indirekte-rede`).
- **Reception is trained by reading style.** Global, selective and detailed reading and listening
  (§4.2.1) are the gist and detail rungs of the task ladder in
  [ADR 0016](../adrs/0016-lessons-are-tasks-and-a-cast-carries-them.md).
- **Production trains the six functions** of §4.2.2: describing, giving an opinion, taking a
  stance, giving examples, expressing possibilities and comparing. Each outcome names one of them.
- **Every unit is in the task shape of ADR 0016 from B2.1**, with one scenario and 2–4
  observable *Ich kann …* outcomes. The shape is the task set of [`item-authoring.md`](../authoring/item-authoring.md#task-sets).
- **Explanations stay German-first**, as at B1
  ([ADR 0005](../adrs/0005-explanation-languages.md)).

## Vocabulary

B2 decks keep B1's two-tier rule ([`a2-b1.md`](a2-b1.md#vocabulary)): 30–40 entries, the core the
unit teaches for production as `cards: both`, the rest `cards: recognition`, never flipped. A B2
deck holds only words no A1–B1 deck owns; `bun run validate` fails a headword that two decks
own. B2 lexis grows by word formation and by fixed combinations more
than by new roots, so a deck's core leans on noun–preposition and adjective–preposition pairs,
Nomen-Verb-Verbindungen and derived forms, each a card of its own.

## Units

Thirteen units. Each is one topic in the task shape of ADR 0016, authored with the `ship-topic`
skill. **Grammar** names the syllabus entries the unit owns, by their keys in
`data/strukturenlisten/goethe-deutsch-online-b2.yaml`, and what it adds beyond them. All 28 entries
are owned by exactly one unit (2+3+3+2+3+2+2+2+2+2+2+2+1 = 28). **Also claims** names the entries
of the two coursebook syllabi that the same rows cite, as `aspekte:<key>` for
`klett-aspekte-neu-b2.yaml` and `erkundungen:<key>` for `schubert-erkundungen-b2.yaml`: 19 of
*Aspekte*'s 20 and 20 of *Erkundungen*'s 27 are owned by exactly one unit, and
[the eight others](#syllabus-entries-no-b2-unit-owns) are ruled on below. The inventory row and its focus tag
are added by the unit's own change, where their names are decided; only a new B2 tag closes a B2
row, and a recycled B1 tag carries the `deepens` edge. **Themen** names the Prüfungsthemen the
unit's manifest claims; every one of the 14 is claimed by at least one unit. **Function** is the
production function of §4.2.2 the unit trains hardest; each outcome names its own.

**Size.** B1 is 14 contracted units owning 32 grammar points, and six further topics. B2 is 13
units owning 28 syllabus entries, of which twelve deepen a structure a B1 unit already owns
(`adjektivdeklination`, `vergleichssaetze-je-desto`, `relativsaetze-relativpronomen`,
`passiv-mit-modalverb`, `angaben-im-mittelfeld`, `partizip-als-adjektiv`,
`zweiteilige-konnektoren`, `um-ohne-statt-zu`, `temporalsaetze`, `indirekte-rede`,
`kausale-zusammenhaenge`, `fragewoerter-wo-praepositionalpronomen-da`). The load is uneven: three
units own three Goethe entries, and three (B2.3, B2.7, B2.13) also carry a structure no syllabus
names. The two coursebook syllabi mostly name the same structures again; what they added to a
unit is *sollen* for a reported claim (B2.1), negation (B2.3), *es* and purpose (B2.8), and place
and the uses of *werden* (B2.13). **A unit that
proves too heavy when it is authored splits**, and the second half is inserted after it as a unit
of its own; nothing is renumbered. Thirteen is the spine, not the final count of B2 topics.

### B2.1 · `sprache-verstaendigung` — Sprache & Verständigung

**Mission:** report what someone said and how they meant it; clear up a misunderstanding; say that
you see or know something differently.
**Grammar:** `indirekte-rede` — Konjunktiv I produced, with the Konjunktiv II replacement where
the forms coincide; `textzusammenhang-herstellen` — reference words and connectors that make two
sentences one text; *sollen* for a claim the speaker passes on without vouching for it (*Er soll
gekündigt haben*). Deepens B1's receptive `indirekte-rede`.
**Also claims:** `aspekte:indirekte-rede-konjunktiv-1`, `aspekte:textzusammenhang`,
`erkundungen:konjunktiv-1-indirekte-rede`, `erkundungen:sollen-weitergabe-von-informationen`.
**Themen:** Sprache, Kommunikation. **Function:** Meinungen äußern.
**Sprachhandlungen owed from B1:** Wissensdivergenz and interkulturelle Begegnung (CON-2) get
their outcomes here.
**Exclude:** journalistic distance markers beyond *laut*, *zufolge* and *sollen*; *wollen* for a
claim about oneself (C1); minutes and protocols; Konjunktiv I outside reported speech.

### B2.2 · `beziehungen-generationen` — Beziehungen & Generationen

**Mission:** describe a relationship and what it rests on; summarise a discussion about family or
friendship; say what you feel about it.
**Grammar:** `adjektivdeklination` — the whole paradigm as one system, including after
*einige, viele, alle, manche*; `nomen-mit-praepositionen`; `modalpartikeln` — *doch, ja, eben,
halt, wohl, eigentlich* understood, and *doch, ja, mal* produced.
**Also claims:** `aspekte:nomen-verben-adjektive-mit-praepositionen`, `aspekte:modalpartikeln`,
`erkundungen:deklination-der-adjektive`.
**Themen:** Persönliche Daten und Verhältnisse. **Function:** beschreiben.
**Sprachhandlungen owed from B1:** Gefühle ausdrücken (CON-2).
**Exclude:** particles as a full inventory, regional particles, psychology vocabulary.

### B2.3 · `gesundheit-lebensstil` — Gesundheit & Lebensstil

**Mission:** explain causes and effects of a habit; take a position on a health offer; advise.
**Grammar:** `kausale-zusammenhaenge` — one cause three ways, as clause, adverb and
prepositional phrase (*weil · deshalb · wegen/aufgrund*), which is where the noun style of B2
starts; `vergleichssaetze-je-desto`; `negation-un-los`, with the negation words beside the
prefix (*kaum, keineswegs, weder … noch*) and where *nicht* stands.
**Also claims:** `aspekte:negation`, `aspekte:vergleichssaetze-als-wie-je-desto`,
`aspekte:nominalisierung-von-verben`, `erkundungen:kausal-und-konsekutivangaben`.
**Themen:** Gesundheit und Hygiene; Ernährung. **Function:** Stellung nehmen.
**Exclude:** medical terminology, diagnoses, nominalisation as a general transformation (each
later unit adds its own relation).

### B2.4 · `gesellschaft-engagement` — Gesellschaft & Engagement

**Mission:** present a project or an initiative; summarise information about participation.
**Grammar:** `passiv-praesens-vergangenheit` — the passive in every tense, and the
Zustandspassiv beside it; `relativsaetze-relativpronomen` — the genitive (*dessen, deren*) and
relative clauses with a preposition, produced.
**Also claims:** `aspekte:passiv-mit-sein`, `erkundungen:das-passiv`.
**Themen:** Beziehungen zu anderen Menschen, Kultur, Tradition. **Function:** Beispiele geben.
**Exclude:** party politics, the passive with modal verbs (B2.5), *lassen* as a passive
substitute (B2.7).

### B2.5 · `wohnen-stadt` — Wohnen & Stadt

**Mission:** apply for a flat; write a formal complaint about a defect; compare ways of living.
**Grammar:** `generalisierende-relativsaetze` — *wer …, der …* and *was*;
`passiv-mit-modalverb` — also in the subordinate clause; `angaben-im-mittelfeld` — the order of
adverbials and where a new or stressed one moves.
**Also claims:** `aspekte:wortstellung-im-satz`, `aspekte:relativsaetze-mit-wer`,
`erkundungen:wortstellung-im-satz`.
**Themen:** Wohnen, Umwelt; Orte. **Function:** vergleichen.
**Exclude:** tenancy law as content, the Nachfeld and Ausklammerung.

### B2.6 · `medien-digital` — Medien & Digitales

**Mission:** discuss advantages and drawbacks; write a comment on an article.
**Grammar:** `partizip-als-adjektiv` — extended participle attributes read, simple ones
produced; `zweiteilige-konnektoren` — *weder … noch, sowohl … als auch, einerseits …
andererseits* beside B1's three.
**Also claims:** `aspekte:zweiteilige-konnektoren`, `aspekte:partizipien-als-adjektive`,
`erkundungen:partizipien-als-adjektive`, `erkundungen:erweiterte-partizipien`.
**Themen:** Freizeit, Unterhaltung. **Function:** Stellung nehmen.
**Exclude:** the Gerundiv (*zu* + Partizip I), technical IT vocabulary.

### B2.7 · `wissenschaft-bildung` — Wissenschaft & Bildung

**Mission:** present a person or an institution; report a study and what follows from it.
**Grammar:** `passiversatzformen` — *sich lassen* + infinitive, *sein* + *zu* + infinitive,
*-bar*; `konsekutive-zusammenhaenge` — *sodass, so … dass, folglich, infolge*.
**Also claims:** `aspekte:passiv-und-passiversatzformen`,
`erkundungen:passiv-und-passiversatzformen`, `erkundungen:wortbildung-der-nomen`.
**Themen:** Erziehung, Ausbildung, Lernen. **Function:** Möglichkeiten ausdrücken.
**Exclude:** academic writing conventions, citation, *zu … als dass*.

### B2.8 · `konsum-gestaltung` — Konsum & Gestaltung

**Mission:** describe and judge an object; give an opinion on a trend or a rule of dress.
**Grammar:** `infinitivsaetze` — the infinitive clause against the *dass* clause, with a
correlate (*darauf …, zu …*) and in the past (*… zu haben*), and *es* as that correlate and as
the placeholder in first position (*Es ist wichtig, … zu …*; *Es kamen viele Gäste.*);
`um-ohne-statt-zu` — with the *dass* variants for a different subject, and purpose three ways
(*um … zu · damit · zu/für* + noun).
**Also claims:** `aspekte:das-wort-es`, `aspekte:um-ohne-statt-zu-und-alternativen`,
`erkundungen:finalangaben`.
**Themen:** Konsum, Handel. **Function:** beschreiben.
**Exclude:** advertising analysis as a genre, *haben/sein* + *zu* as modal substitutes (B2.7).

### B2.9 · `arbeitswelt` — Arbeitswelt

**Mission:** write an official message; describe working conditions; build a professional
profile.
**Grammar:** `modalsaetze-indem-dadurch-dass` — with *durch* + noun beside them;
`adjektive-mit-praeposition`; Nomen-Verb-Verbindungen beyond B1.4's set, as the register of
official messages.
**Also claims:** `aspekte:nomen-verb-verbindungen`, `aspekte:modalsaetze-dadurch-dass-indem`,
`erkundungen:nomen-verb-verbindungen`, `erkundungen:modalangaben`.
**Themen:** tägliches Leben, Arbeit; Dienstleistungen. **Function:** beschreiben.
**Exclude:** labour law, salary negotiation, the vocational Handlungsfelder of the BAMF catalogue
as a programme.

### B2.10 · `kunst-kultur` — Kunst & Kultur

**Mission:** present a place or a work; say what you think of it and concede what speaks
against.
**Grammar:** `konzessive-zusammenhaenge` — *obwohl · trotzdem/dennoch · trotz*;
`temporalsaetze` — the full set with *sobald, solange, seitdem, bis, ehe*, and their sequence of
tenses.
**Also claims:** `aspekte:waehrend-praepositionen-mit-genitiv`, `erkundungen:konzessivangaben`.
**Themen:** Beziehungen zu anderen Menschen, Kultur, Tradition. **Function:** Meinungen äußern.
**Exclude:** art-historical vocabulary, literary analysis.

### B2.11 · `gefuehle-wahrnehmung` — Gefühle & Wahrnehmung

**Mission:** summarise a report; talk about an experience and how it felt; contrast two views.
**Grammar:** `irreale-vergleichssaetze` — *als ob, als wenn, als* + verb, with the Konjunktiv II
of the past they need, and the unreal condition and wish in the past (*hätte … gemacht*, with a
modal *hätte … machen können*); `adversative-verbindungen` — *während, wohingegen, dagegen,
jedoch*.
**Also claims:** `aspekte:vergleichssaetze-als-ob-konjunktiv-2`,
`erkundungen:konjunktiv-2-hoeflichkeit-irrealitaet`.
**Themen:** Gesundheit und Hygiene. **Function:** vergleichen.
**Exclude:** Konjunktiv II in reported speech (B2.1), *zu … als dass*.

### B2.12 · `reisen-mobilitaet` — Reisen & Mobilität

**Mission:** report from experience; give a short talk; complain about a service.
**Grammar:** `konditionale-zusammenhaenge` — *falls, sofern, es sei denn, andernfalls, bei*;
`fragewoerter-wo-praepositionalpronomen-da` — *wo(r)-* and *da(r)-*, and *da(r)-* announcing a
clause.
**Also claims:** `erkundungen:konditionalangaben`, `erkundungen:verben-mit-praepositionalem-kasus`.
**Themen:** Reise. **Function:** Möglichkeiten ausdrücken.
**Exclude:** travel law, booking transactions already owned by B1.8.

### B2.13 · `wandel-zukunft` — Wandel & Zukunft

**Mission:** describe how a region or a building changed; say what will probably happen and how
sure you are.
**Grammar:** `temporale-praepositionen-adverbien`; where something is and where it moved, with
relative clauses in *wo, wohin, woher*; *werden* in its three uses — the full verb of change, the
future as a prediction, and the passive — told apart. Certainty is graded with the future and
adverbs (*wohl, vermutlich, wahrscheinlich, sicher*); the modal verbs of guessing are understood
only (see Beyond the syllabus).
**Also claims:** `erkundungen:temporalangaben`, `erkundungen:lokalangaben`,
`erkundungen:funktionen-von-werden`.
**Themen:** Klima. **Function:** Möglichkeiten ausdrücken.
**Exclude:** climate science terminology, political programmes.

### Beyond the syllabus

A reference grammar's B2 scope is wider than three courses' lesson lists. The units below take
what none of the three syllabi names, each as an inventory row that
`bun scripts/structures.ts B2 --beyond` lists; a row here is this course's decision and cites no
source.

| Structure | Unit | Why there, and how far |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Modal verbs for a guess (*dürfte, müsste, könnte*) and Futur II for a guess about the past | B2.13 | the exam's reading texts use them; **understood, never produced**. *Erkundungen* teaches the guessing meaning in its C1 book |
| Adjective suffixes beyond the ones a syllabus names (*-reich, -arm, -voll* beside *un-, -los, -bar*) | B2.3, B2.7 | they arrive with the vocabulary of the unit |

Structures that B2 courses sometimes teach and that stay out: the Gerundiv, the Nachfeld,
*zu … als dass*, and *wollen* for a claim about oneself. They are C1 here.

### Syllabus entries no B2 unit owns

Eight entries of the two coursebook syllabi are not owned by a B2 unit. Seven are revision in
those books: an A1–B1 inventory row already owns the structure and cites the entry, so the report
shows it claimed at the level this course delivers it (`bun scripts/structures.ts B2`).

| Entry | Owned by | Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `aspekte:indefinitpronomen` | `indefinitpronomen-erweitert` | B1 |
| `erkundungen:vergangenheitsformen-der-verben` | `praeteritum-vollverben` | B1 |
| `erkundungen:modalverben` | `modalverben` | A1 |
| `erkundungen:n-deklination-der-nomen` | `n-deklination` | B1 |
| `erkundungen:rektion-der-verben` | `verbrektion-kasus` | A2 |

heads a mixed exercise section and names no structure.

### Order

The path follows what each unit needs from the ones before. B2.1 comes first because the produced
reported speech is the one B2 structure B1 already prepared. B2.3 opens the three-way statement of
a relation that B2.7, B2.9, B2.10 and B2.12 repeat for consequence, means, concession and
condition. B2.4 precedes B2.5 and B2.7, which extend its passive. B2.11 follows B2.10 because the
contrast is taught against the concession.

# B1–B2 curriculum contract

What B2 teaches, which unit owns what, and what each unit may not touch. **No B2 unit is
contracted yet.** This file holds the anchors and the rules that every B2 unit inherits. A unit is
added under [Units](#units) when it is contracted, in path order, in the format of
[`a2-b1.md`](a2-b1.md#b1) (Mission, Grammar, Exclude). The identity rules there apply unchanged:
an id is frozen the moment it ships.

## Anchors

DTZ ends at B1, so none of the DTZ Themen, Sprachhandlungen or Strukturen lists reach B2. B2's
denominators come from the Goethe-Zertifikat B2 and from what it points to. The official files are
local and gitignored ([ADR 0009](../adrs/0009-third-party-and-official-material.md)), and this
table records labels only.

| Anchor | What it gives B2 | Held |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goethe-Zertifikat B2 *Prüfungsziele, Testbeschreibung* (2007 handbook) | 14 Prüfungsthemen (§4.3); reading and listening styles (§4.2.1); production functions (§4.2.2); what marks B2 against B1 (§4.5) | `docs/GeotheInstitute/`. It describes the earlier exam, and the 2025 Modellsatz cites a newer edition (P26-7) |
| Goethe-Zertifikat B2 *Modellsatz Erwachsene* (2nd ed., 2025) | the current task formats of Lesen, Hören, Schreiben and Sprechen | `docs/GeotheInstitute/` |
| *Profile deutsch* (2005) | B2 grammar and vocabulary. §4.4 of the *Prüfungsziele* names it as the inventory of linguistic means | not held (P26-7) |

Until *Profile deutsch* is held, B2 has no lexical or structural denominator. No B2 coverage figure
is published, and `bun scripts/structures.ts` reports B2 rows as `beyond`.

The 14 Prüfungsthemen are the Themen denominator: Persönliche Daten und Verhältnisse; Wohnen,
Umwelt; tägliches Leben, Arbeit; Freizeit, Unterhaltung; Reise; Beziehungen zu anderen Menschen,
Kultur, Tradition; Gesundheit und Hygiene; Erziehung, Ausbildung, Lernen; Konsum, Handel;
Ernährung; Dienstleistungen; Orte; Sprache, Kommunikation; Klima. They enter
`data/themenlisten/` as one file whose entries start `unclaimed` (CON-8).

## Progression principles

- **B2 grows abstraction and nuance, not paradigm count.** The *Prüfungsziele* name three marks of
  B2 against B1 (§4.5): effective argumentation, staying power in discourse, and language
  awareness. Texts move beyond the concrete everyday topics of B1 to abstract ones.
- **B2 deepens B1 and never re-teaches it.** `deepens` edges and cumulative retrieval work as in
  B1. A structure that B1 holds receptively is produced at B2, starting with Konjunktiv I in
  reported speech (`data/grammar-inventory.yaml`, row `indirekte-rede`).
- **Reception is trained by reading style.** Global, selective and detailed reading and listening
  (§4.2.1) are the gist and detail rungs of the task ladder in
  [ADR 0016](../adrs/0016-lessons-are-tasks-and-a-cast-carries-them.md).
- **Production trains the six functions** of §4.2.2: describing, giving an opinion, taking a
  stance, giving examples, expressing possibilities and comparing. Each outcome names one of them.
- **Every unit is in the task shape of ADR 0016 from B2.1**, with one scenario and 2–4
  observable *Ich kann …* outcomes. The shape is the task set of [`item-authoring.md`](../authoring/item-authoring.md#task-sets).
- **Explanations stay German-first**, as at B1
  ([ADR 0005](../adrs/0005-explanation-languages.md)).

## Vocabulary

Goethe publishes no B2 Wortliste. B2 decks keep B1's two-tier rule
([`a2-b1.md`](a2-b1.md#vocabulary)), until this contract says otherwise. A deck makes no coverage
claim until the *Profile deutsch* lexis is imported as an instrument.

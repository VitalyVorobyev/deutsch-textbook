# Approved brief — B1 simulated job advertisement

Date: 2026-07-28
Selected by: Vitaly Vorobyev
Implementation assistance: OpenAI Codex

## Authentic-document job

Replace a vacancy quoted inside a multiple-choice prompt with a stable,
course-created job-ad document. The learner must extract and keep separate:

- `Ihre Aufgaben`;
- `Voraussetzungen`;
- `Wir bieten`;
- contact and application details.

## Selected composition

Create a portrait-format advertisement for the fictional `Nordlicht Markt
GmbH`, seeking a colleague for a part-time sales role from September. Preserve
the existing scored facts: the company requires sales experience and good
German and offers a part-time position plus annual training.

The layout must use deterministic SVG so every word and grouping remains
controllable. Section headings, borders, spacing, icons and a complete
EN/RU/UK/DE description must carry the structure without depending on colour.
The document remains visible beside its exercise and has a full German text
transcript. It is a simulated teaching document, not a real vacancy, and
viewing it creates no learner evidence.
